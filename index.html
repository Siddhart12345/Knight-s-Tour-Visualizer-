Notes

Warnsdorff's Heuristic

A greedy rule published in 1823: at each square, move to the unvisited square with the fewest onward knight moves. By prioritising the least accessible squares, it avoids trapping the knight early and solves most boards in near-linear time.

Backtracking (DFS)

Systematically tests all eight knight moves in a fixed order. When trapped, it unwinds the last move (red indicator) and tests the next branch. Complete and optimal, but worst-case exponential on boards larger than 6 × 6.

Hybrid

Sorts candidate moves by Warnsdorff degree before exploring them with depth-first search, combining greedy speed with a completeness guarantee.
